BEHIND THE VIEW

Clear data. Clear limits.

This is Echo’s simulated dataset, not official platform analytics. The timelines differ, so the comparison starts at observation day 1. Metrics and correlations update for the selected period. Results are descriptive, not causal.

What happened to the raw files?

Each original file has 1,005 rows. The reproducible cleaning removes duplicates and invalid dates; Threads drops missing records while TikTok imputes missing DAU and churn with medians. The study also replaces non-positive DAU with the positive mean and takes absolute negative churn. These are educational assumptions.
